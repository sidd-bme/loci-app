# Loci product foundation

Updated for the image-first implementation, 8 September 2026. Exact implemented
subsets and qualification status live in [CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md](CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md)
and [PROJECT_STATE.md](PROJECT_STATE.md).

## Product promise

A life-science researcher can open an image, understand its available structure,
explore its detail, and discover appropriate annotation, processing,
segmentation, measurement and export tools. Saving a study helps organise work;
it is not a prerequisite for viewing. The Apache-2.0 core remains usable locally
without an account, commercial service or model download.

The scope spans ordinary microscopy, fluorescence, histology/whole slides,
volumetric microscopy, time series and supported medical data. Verified axes,
sample semantics, calibration and declared interpretation guide the interface.
File extensions and visual appearance never establish biological identity.

## One workflow

1. Open images, a folder, an explicit series or a supported directory store.
2. Explore the full available extent with bounded, progressive rendering.
3. Draw source-bound annotations before segmentation when useful.
4. Choose an explicit analysis, inspect the declared region/settings and run.
5. Correct and review derived results; retain uncertainty and provenance.
6. Export a rendered view or reviewed scientific result with its meaning clear.
7. Reopen an autosaved session or save a named study without copying originals.

Sources/results remain on the left, the image dominates the centre, and four
predictable task groups occupy the inspector. Advanced tools remain searchable.
Source, selected result, geometry and review state stay visibly connected.

## Interaction and visual direction

Use Loci's graphite, paper and mineral-teal family for interface hierarchy.
Scientific canvases have neutral backgrounds and no decorative pixel filters.
Readable density, consistent spacing, restrained borders and distinct selected,
hover, focus, pressed and disabled states matter more than decorative motion.

The welcome mark responds to activation with up to six bounded optical phase
pulses while its hit area remains stable. Optional opening choices reveal
downwards from a stable anchor. Recent work shows locally recoverable sessions,
and keyboard-navigable research tips link directly to supported source routes.
Other motion is short and interruptible; reduced motion removes nonessential
effects. Scientific images, labels and numerical changes are never interpolated
for decoration.

Supplementary help appears once on hover or keyboard focus and dismisses on
activation, exit or Escape. The bottom bar carries operational status. Required
calibration, review, destructive-action, export and transfer information remains
visible. Missing studies and unavailable capabilities offer concise recovery
steps with redacted technical details.

## Evidence and data boundaries

Original sources are immutable. Derived state retains exact source and model
identities, geometry, settings, corrections, reviews and artifact hashes.
Camera navigation never changes the analysis region or measurements. Display
colour transformations operate on display copies; RGB samples are not
biological channels. Raw-volume rendering and the separate bounded Surface
preview are display-only. Surface can inspect an explicit source Z range or an
exact three-dimensional result; it does not create a quantitative measurement,
segmentation or adopted result.

Tests and actual packaged journeys establish engineering behavior for their
identified fixtures and builds. They do not establish biological accuracy,
clinical utility, scanner-wide fidelity, human usability or universal hardware
performance. Additional specialist methods enter only with justified use cases,
maintained implementations, resource/rights review and executable acceptance.

## Publication and commercial direction

The first publication route is a software paper after public-release permission,
rights review and independent research use. [JOSS submission guidance](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html)
and [review criteria](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/review_criteria.html),
checked 8 September 2026, require a public repository, substantive research
software, documentation/tests, demonstrated research use and more than six
months of public development history. JOSS charges no publication fee. Private
development and engineering role-play do not satisfy those history/use gates.
Disclose AI assistance under the journal's current policy and retain accountable
human authorship. A later workflow paper needs predefined representative tasks,
independent researchers, task time/error rates and reproducible comparisons;
biological accuracy needs separate reference labels and evaluation.

The near-term commercial route is paid implementation and support around the
free local core. [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
permits charging for support, warranty and related obligations. Propose a small,
fixed-fee Cellivate pilot covering installation, a declared workflow,
qualification protocol, onboarding and a bounded support period, with acceptance
criteria and a stop/go review before expansion. Cellivate's
[pilot offering](https://www.cellivatetech.com/pilot) provides relevant workflow
context; it does not establish that the company buys imaging software, a budget
or an agreed partnership. Dependency and checkpoint rights remain separate from
Loci's core licence.

A website referral can link to Loci and offer an optional, separately consented
Cellivate contact form. Local use must not depend on contact sharing, image
uploads or silent telemetry. Before collecting leads, agree purpose, fields,
consent/withdrawal, access, retention and deletion under the applicable
[Singapore PDPA obligations](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act/data-protection-obligations).
Resolve revenue attribution, payout timing, support ownership, IP and termination
in a written agreement. These are proposals: no customer contact, billing,
lead collection, public release or earnings projection is implemented here.
