Specimens and image credits

These are real research specimens. The interactive fluorescence example uses windowed display values. The desktop views come from Loci running on public datasets. Each dataset has its own terms.

Human stem-cell colony

Derek Thirstrup and the Microscopy & Image Analysis Team at the Allen Institute for Cell Science contributed BBBC034v1 (2018) to the Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection. The interactive specimen uses field AICS_12_134. Amber shows the plasma membrane, aqua shows mEGFP-tagged α-tubulin, and violet shows DNA. The fluorescence workbench view shows a maximum-intensity projection over native sections 15–20 inclusive. The depth control shows individual sections.

The acquisition has 0.065 µm XY spacing and a 0.29 µm Z step. The browser uses three independently windowed 8-bit display channels. The colony-center measurement example comes from a separate acquisition with EGFP β-actin and Hoechst. Source and contributor credit · Cell line · BBBC publication · CC BY 4.0 · Display settings

Segmentation and cell measurements

The three registered panels show the same 256 × 256 crop on native section 40 of the BBBC034v1 colony-center acquisition. Cellpose-SAM v2 used the membrane channel. The right panel shows EGFP β-actin and the outline of predicted object 6. Its mean comes from the original 16-bit intensities. The independent display windows, source indices and model attribution accompany the figure. Figure record · Dataset and contributors · CC BY 4.0

The 11-second measurement film uses a different set of 38 unreviewed predictions on the colony-center field. The selected object is number 18 in that result. Both short films and their posters were recorded in the Apple silicon development workbench, build 79, for this website.

Confocal plant volume

Célia Baroux, Ethel Mendocilla-Sato and Daphné Autran deposited the wild-type Arabidopsis thaliana ovule for the study by Hernández-Lagana and colleagues. The four-pane workbench view and the 10-second volume film show the EM_C_81 acquisition. Loci used normal shading and a teal display color. Dataset and CC0 terms

Whole-slide histology

CMU-1 is an Aperio slide distributed by the OpenSlide project. Loci rendered the selected tissue region from the 46,000 × 32,914 pixel slide. OpenSlide data and CC0 terms

Linked anatomical MRI

The ICBM152 2009b nonlinear asymmetric T1 template is a population average with 0.5 mm isotropic spacing. Credit to Louis Collins, the McConnell Brain Imaging Centre, the Montreal Neurological Institute and McGill University. Dataset · Fonov et al., 2011 · Fonov et al., 2009

Organoid lineages

Max Betjes and colleagues released the time-lapse movie and its OrganoidTracker 2.0 graph. Loci displays the imported positions, daughter links and probability fields on the original time series. Dataset and CC BY 4.0 terms · Method

Digital holographic microscopy

The phase image is the cell example from Paul Müller, Mirjam Schürmann, Salvatore Girardo, Gheorghe Cojoc and Jochen Guck. Loci read the original intensities and 0.107 µm pixel spacing. Original study · Image and CC0 terms

U2OS nuclei

Juan C. Caicedo and colleagues contributed the Hoechst-labelled U2OS images in BBBC039v1. The workbench comparison uses A06, site 6. It compares two segmentation predictions on that field. Source and CC0 terms · Nuclei-boundary model

Application, model examples and film
